# Audit Pup on a Chromebook: Quick Guide for Tax-Aide Counselors

Audit Pup checks the TaxSlayer Practice Lab PDF for one step of a training case against the answer key. It runs entirely in Chrome. Nothing is uploaded.

**Open it here:** https://jaycarterwells.github.io/audit-pup/ (tip: press **Ctrl + D** to bookmark it)

## Before you start

* Sign in to your Chromebook with your own account and use a normal Chrome window. **In Guest mode or an Incognito window, your progress is erased when you close the window**, with no warning.
* Use the Chrome browser. Do not open Audit Pup from a Google Drive preview.

## Meet the dogs

* **Zeke** is Audit Pup's chief inspector. His speech bubble at the top of the page tells you what to do next.
* Next to your score, one of three dogs shows how the step went, with its title under its name:
  * **Zion, corrections officer** (red ring): something needs fixing.
  * **Bailey, follow-up clerk** (yellow ring): there is more to do, or a warning is showing.
  * **Zeke, chief inspector** (green ring): the step is done.

## 1. Save the PDF from TaxSlayer

After you finish a step in TaxSlayer, print the full return.

* If the return opens in a new tab, click the **Download** arrow at the top right.
* If a print window opens, set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**, then click **Save**.
* Save it to **Downloads** with a clear name that includes the case and the step.

Print a **new** PDF after every step. Audit Pup warns you if you reuse the same file for a later step.

## 2. Check the step

1. Open Audit Pup. It opens on the first step you have not finished. Make sure the **case** and the **step** match the step you just finished in TaxSlayer.
2. Click the box that names your step, for example **Drop your Step A PDF here**. The Files app opens. Go to **Downloads**, pick your PDF, and click **Open**. (You can also drag the file from the Files app anywhere onto the page.) The file name appears in bold after **Selected:**.
3. Click the button that names your step, for example **Check Step A**.

When a step is done, Audit Pup selects the **next step** for you and clears the old file. Zeke names the next step at the top of the page. When every step of the case is done, Zeke says so.

## 3. Read the results

* The score shows how many checks passed. It stays in view while you scroll, along with the file you checked and the step it was checked against.
* The bar under the score is **red** when something needs fixing, **yellow** while items are left to finish or a warning is showing, and **green** when the step is done. The dog next to the score matches the bar.
* Each line shows its status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Correct** | Matches the answer key. Your amount is shown. |
| **Check** | Does not match. Your amount is shown. |
| **Changed** | On a no-change step, this line moved since the earlier step. |
| **Form missing** | A form the answer key checks is not in your PDF. |
| **Unread** | Audit Pup could not find this line. Compare it by hand. |
| **To do** | An item you still need to finish yourself. |
| **Confirmed** | An item you checked off yourself. |

* On a line that needs another look, click **Show a hint** to see the answer key value and where to look in the problem.
* **Worksheet entries:** some steps ask you to type values from a worksheet you completed, and to pick the option you chose. Type them in the boxes shown.
* **Items you confirm yourself:** some items, such as a TS Note, are not printed in the PDF. Check the box yourself once you have done it. Click **See an example note** for a sample.
* **No-change steps:** some steps should show **no change** from an earlier step. The step picker names the step it is compared to.
* **Did you pick the right step?** A yellow box appears if your PDF looks like it is from a later step than the one you picked. Click the button in the box to check against the right step.
* **Your reward:** when every check passes, every item is finished, and no warning is showing, a random dog photo appears in an **Approved!** snapshot. Tap it for another dog. Without internet you see a green check mark instead.

To fix something: go back to TaxSlayer, correct it, print a new PDF, and check again.

## Your progress

The paw-print trail under the step picker shows each step as **Done**, **Fix**, **To finish**, or **Not checked**. The selected step has a gold ring. Cases with many steps wrap onto two or three rows. Tap a step to select it.

Progress is saved **only in Chrome on this Chromebook**. It does not follow you to another computer, and clearing your browsing history erases it. **Start over** (above the trail, on the right) erases it on purpose. Audit Pup asks first.

## Handy Chromebook keys

| To do this | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Make text bigger or smaller | **Ctrl +** or **Ctrl -** (reset with **Ctrl 0**) |
| Switch between TaxSlayer and Audit Pup tabs | **Ctrl + Tab** |
| Open the Files app | **Alt + Shift + M** |
| Reload the page | **Ctrl + R** |

The **Theme** button switches between light and dark. The **Version** button shows what changed in each version. It has a **New** tag the first time you open a new version.

## If something goes wrong

* **"That file is not a PDF"**: save the return from TaxSlayer as a PDF and try again.
* **"Could not read that PDF"**: make sure you picked the PDF printed from TaxSlayer for this step, then try again.
* **"This is the same file you checked for Step B"** (or another step): you are reusing an older PDF. Print a new PDF from TaxSlayer after finishing the current step, then check that one.
* **"Audit Pup did not finish loading"**: reload the page with **Ctrl + R**.
* **"Audit Pup did not start"**: you are seeing a preview. Open the link above in Chrome instead.
* **"This browser is not saving progress"**: your browser is blocking saved data. Use a normal Chrome window, signed in to your own account.
* **No dog photo**: the reward photo needs internet. Checking still works without it.
