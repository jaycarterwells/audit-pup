# Audit Pup on a Chromebook: Quick Guide for Tax-Aide Counselors

Audit Pup checks the TaxSlayer Practice Lab PDF for one step of a training case against the answer key. It runs entirely in Chrome. Nothing is uploaded.

You can open this guide at any time with the **Users Guide** button at the top of Audit Pup. Click **Close** (or press Esc) to go back.

**Open it here:** https://jaycarterwells.github.io/audit-pup/ (tip: press **Ctrl + D** to bookmark it)

## Before you start

* Sign in to your Chromebook with your own account and use a normal Chrome window. **In Guest mode or an Incognito window, your progress is erased when you close the window**, with no warning.
* Use the Chrome browser. Do not open Audit Pup from a Google Drive preview.

## Meet the dogs

* **Zeke** is Audit Pup's chief inspector. His speech bubble at the top of the page tells you what to do next.
* Next to your score, one of three dogs shows how the step went, with its title under its name:
  * **Zion, corrections officer** (red ring): something needs fixing.
  * **Bailey, follow-up clerk** (yellow ring): there is more to do, or a warning is showing.
  * **Zeke, chief inspector** (green ring): the step is done.

## 1. Save the PDF from TaxSlayer

After you finish a step in TaxSlayer, print the full return.

* If the return opens in a new tab, click the **Download** arrow at the top right.
* If a print window opens, set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**, then click **Save**.
* Save it to **Downloads** with a clear name that includes the case and the step.

Print a **new** PDF after every step. Audit Pup warns you if you reuse the same file for a later step.

## 2. Check the step

1. Open Audit Pup. The box at the top names the step Audit Pup expects, for example **Drop your Step A PDF here**. Click **Choose PDF**. The Files app opens. Go to **Downloads**, pick your PDF, and click **Open**. (You can also drag the file from the Files app onto the box, or anywhere on the page.) The box then shrinks to one gray bar with your file name in bold after **Selected:**. To use another file, click **Choose a different PDF**.
2. Audit Pup reads the name on the Form 1040 and picks the **case** for you. If the PDF is for a different case than the one showing, it switches to that case, and Zeke tells you, for example: "This PDF is from the Graham case, so I switched from Quinn." Each case keeps its own progress.
3. Under the box, check that the **step** is the step you just finished in TaxSlayer. Audit Pup opens on the first step you have not finished. To pick another step, use the step box or tap a paw in your progress.
4. Click the button that names your step, for example **Check Step A**. It is gray and says **Choose a PDF first** until you choose a PDF, then it turns gold with an arrow.

When a step is done, Audit Pup selects the **next step** for you and clears the old file. Zeke names the next step at the top of the page. When you finish the last step of a case, Zeke congratulates you. If some earlier steps were never checked in Audit Pup, he names the first of them, and you can check it whenever you like. When every step of the case is done, Zeke says so.

## 3. Read the results

* The score shows how many checks passed. It stays in view while you scroll, along with the file you checked and the step it was checked against.
* The bar under the score is **red** when something needs fixing, **yellow** while items are left to finish or a warning is showing, and **green** when the step is done. The dog next to the score matches the bar.
* Each line shows its status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Correct** | Matches the answer key. Your amount is shown. |
| **Check** | Does not match. Your amount is shown. |
| **Changed** | On a no-change step, this line moved since the earlier step. |
| **Form missing** | A form the answer key checks is not in your PDF. |
| **Unread** | Audit Pup could not find this line. Compare it by hand. |
| **To do** | An item you still need to finish yourself. |
| **Confirmed** | An item you checked off yourself. |

* On a line that needs another look, click **Show a hint** to see the answer key value and where to look in the problem.
* **Lines that should be blank:** the lines that should have nothing on them yet at your step are checked together, in one row at the end of the list, together with any box that should not be checked. If any of them has an amount or a check mark, that row lists each one, and its hint shows the answer key as **blank or $0**.
* **Notes Audit Pup checks for you:** some answer key notes, such as an Identity Protection PIN, a Preparer Note, the address on a W-2, the Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet, or estimated tax vouchers, are read from your PDF. They show **Correct** or **Check** like any other line.
* **Notes from earlier steps:** some notes, such as a Preparer Note or your filing status, must stay in the return once they are added. At later steps they are checked again, together in one row. If one is missing, that row names it and the step it was added at. Items you confirm yourself, such as a TS Note box, are not asked about again.
* **Worksheet entries:** some steps ask you to type values from a worksheet you completed, and to pick the option you chose. Type them in the boxes shown.
* **Items you confirm yourself:** some items, such as a TS Note, are not printed in the PDF. Check the box yourself once you have done it. Click **See an example note** for a sample.
* **No-change steps:** some steps should show **no change** from an earlier step. The step picker names the step it is compared to.
* **Did you pick the right step?** If your PDF does not pass the step you picked but matches a different step (for example, the PDF you printed for the previous step), a yellow box names that step. Print a new PDF after finishing your step, or click the button in the box to check against the step it matches. Your progress for the step you picked does not change.
* **Your reward:** when every check passes, every item is finished, and no warning is showing, a random dog photo appears in an **Approved!** snapshot. Tap it for another dog. Without internet you see a green check mark instead.

To fix something: go back to TaxSlayer, correct it, print a new PDF, and check again.

## Your progress

The paw-print trail under the Check button shows each step by its letter, with its status under the paw: **Done**, **Fix**, or **To finish**. A step with nothing under its letter is not checked yet. The selected step has a gold ring. Cases with many steps wrap onto two rows. Tap a step to select it. Point at a paw (or listen with a screen reader) for its full status and when it was checked.

Each case keeps its own progress, so you can switch cases and come back. Progress is saved **only in Chrome on this Chromebook**. It does not follow you to another computer, and clearing your browsing history erases it. The link above the trail, on the right, names the case you have picked, for example "Start Kent Over". It erases the progress for that case only, on purpose: Audit Pup first asks you to confirm erasing that case, and your other cases are never changed.

## If something goes wrong

* **"That file is not a PDF"**: save the return from TaxSlayer as a PDF and try again.
* **"Could not read that PDF"**: make sure you picked the PDF printed from TaxSlayer for this step, then try again.
* **"This is the same file you checked for Step B"** (or another step): you are reusing an older PDF. Print a new PDF from TaxSlayer after finishing the current step, then check that one.
* **"Please update Audit Pup before you check a PDF"**: a newer version is out, and Audit Pup will not check a PDF until you update, so no one checks against an old answer key. Click **Update**. The page reloads with the newest version in a few seconds and keeps your progress; then choose your PDF again.
* **"This copy of Audit Pup is out of date"**: you opened a downloaded copy. Click **Open the Audit Pup website** and use the website, which always has the newest version.
* **"Audit Pup did not finish loading"**: reload the page with **Ctrl + R**.
* **"Audit Pup did not start"**: you are seeing a preview. Open the link above in Chrome instead.
* **"This browser is not saving progress"**: your browser is blocking saved data. Use a normal Chrome window, signed in to your own account.
* **No dog photo**: the reward photo needs internet. Checking still works without it.

## For instructors

Instructor mode compares a counselor's final returns with the golden files, so you see every difference without the manual PDF compare.

1. Click **Instructor** at the bottom of the page and enter the passcode (ask the Tax-Aide instructor lead). Instructor mode stays on in this browser until you click **Leave instructor mode**.
2. Name each PDF with the counselor's initials, a space, and the case name, for example `DH Quinn.pdf` or `DH Kent.pdf`. The report reads the initials from the file names; if they are missing or do not agree, it says **Unknown**.
3. Drop the counselor's Quality Review PDFs on the box, or click **Choose PDFs**. Up to 5 at a time, one per case. Audit Pup picks each file's case from the name on its Form 1040, and says which file and case it is reading and comparing as it works.
4. Read the report. Each case has its own section, in alphabetical order: missing and extra pages first, then one row per difference with the golden value, the counselor's value, and **The spot** (a small picture of that place on the counselor's page, with its PDF page number). A difference printed on several pages, such as an address on every form, is one row that lists its other pages.
5. Differences instructors never comment on (dates after the tax year, the preparer's PTIN, self-select e-file PINs, TaxSlayer's own Link numbers, and a middle initial added or left out) are not rows. First and last names must match exactly. Each case counts them in a closed list you can open.
6. Click **Copy report** to paste the report into your notes or an email.
7. Under each row are suggested comments from the instructors' comment library for that form, the closest ones first (open **More comments** for the rest). Tick a comment to use it; its words open so you can change them. Where a comment says what the counselor entered, the counselor's value is already filled in. Click **Write a comment** to add your own, and pick the step it belongs to.
8. Below each case's table: **Other comments** lists the case's comments for mistakes the PDF does not show, and the **TS Notes checklist** lists every TS Note the case expects. TaxSlayer does not print TS Notes, so open the return in TaxSlayer and mark each one **Found** or **Missing**.
9. Click **Copy feedback letter**: it puts every comment you ticked or wrote, and each missing TS Note, into one letter grouped by case and step, ready to paste into an email. The header says which version of the comment library is in use; a yellow box means the library could not be reached and an older copy is used.

## For beta testers

Audit Pup is in beta until version 1.0, so everyone using it is a beta tester. Thank you. Use it the way you would in class, and tell us whenever something is confusing, wrong, or slow.

Please try as many of these as you can:

* Check a step with your own PDF, and a step you know has a mistake. Does the hint help you find it?
* Check a step with the PDF from the step before. Does the yellow box name the right step?
* Drop a PDF from a different case than the one showing. Does Audit Pup switch to the right case?
* Close Chrome, reopen Audit Pup, and confirm your progress is still there.
* Zoom in with **Ctrl +** to 125% or 150%. Is everything still readable?

**How to report a problem:** tell Carter, who looks after Audit Pup. Carter's contact information is on your class instructor contact list. Include:

1. The version (on the **Version** button, top right) and your device, for example "Chromebook, Chrome".
2. The case and step, and the name of the PDF you checked.
3. What you expected, and what Audit Pup showed (the line, its status, and the score).
4. A screenshot (on a Chromebook, **Ctrl + Show windows**), and if you can, the PDF itself. The practice cases use made-up data, so the PDF is safe to share.

**Most important to report:** any line Audit Pup marks **Correct** when you know it is wrong. Audit Pup is built so that this never happens.

Known limits:

* Audit Pup checks only the lines and notes on the answer key. A mistake that changes no checked line will not be caught.
* TaxSlayer does not print TS Notes, so TS Note boxes and worksheet entries rely on you.
* Some steps have the same answers by design, so their PDFs cannot be told apart. The same-file note covers the usual mix-up.
* Some hints are new and still being reviewed by instructors. If a hint seems wrong or unclear, please report that too.
