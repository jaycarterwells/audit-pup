# Audit Pup on a Chromebook: Quick Guide for Tax-Aide Counselors

Audit Pup checks the TaxSlayer Practice Lab PDF for one step of a training case against the answer key. It runs entirely in Chrome. Nothing is uploaded.

**Open it here:** https://jaycarterwells.github.io/audit-pup/ (tip: press **Ctrl + D** to bookmark it)

## Before you start

* Sign in to your Chromebook with your own account and use a normal Chrome window. **In Guest mode or an Incognito window, your progress is erased when you close the window**, with no warning.
* Use the Chrome browser. Do not open Audit Pup from a Google Drive preview.

## 1. Save the PDF from TaxSlayer

After you finish a step in TaxSlayer, print the full return.

* If the return opens in a new tab, click the **Download** arrow at the top right.
* If a print window opens, set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**, then click **Save**.
* Save it to **Downloads** with a clear name, such as `Quinn-Step-B.pdf`.

Print a **new** PDF after every step. Audit Pup warns you if you reuse the same file for a later step.

## 2. Check the step

1. Open Audit Pup and choose the **case** and the **step** you just finished.
2. Click the box that says **Drop your TaxSlayer PDF here**. The Files app opens. Go to **Downloads**, pick your PDF, and click **Open**. (You can also drag the file from the Files app onto the box.)
3. Click **Check this step**.

## 3. Read the results

* The score at the top shows how many lines match the answer key. It stays in view while you scroll.
* Each line that needs another look has a **hint** with the answer key value.
* Some items, like the **TS Note**, cannot be read from the PDF. Check the box yourself once you have done it.
* Steps D and F should show **no change** from the step before them.
* When everything matches, a dog appears. Tap it for another dog.

To fix something: go back to TaxSlayer, correct it, print a new PDF, and check again.

## Your progress

The row under the step picker shows each step as **Done**, **Fix**, **To finish**, or **Not checked**. Tap a step to jump to it.

Progress is saved **only in Chrome on this Chromebook**. It does not follow you to another computer, and clearing your browsing history erases it. **Start over** erases it on purpose.

## Handy Chromebook keys

| To do this | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Make text bigger or smaller | **Ctrl +** or **Ctrl -** (reset with **Ctrl 0**) |
| Switch between TaxSlayer and Audit Pup tabs | **Ctrl + Tab** |
| Open the Files app | **Alt + Shift + M** |
| Reload the page | **Ctrl + R** |

The **Theme** button switches between light and dark. The **Version** button shows what changed recently.

## If something goes wrong

* **"That file is not a PDF"**: save the return from TaxSlayer as a PDF and try again.
* **"Audit Pup did not start"**: you are seeing a preview. Open the link above in Chrome instead.
* **"This browser is not saving progress"**: this Chromebook is blocking sites from saving data, often because of a school or work setting. Checking still works, but progress resets when you close the page. Ask your site coordinator, or use a different Chromebook.
* **No dog picture**: the dog needs internet. Checking still works without it.
